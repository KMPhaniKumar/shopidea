Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 May 2026  |  Effective date: 19 May 2026

ReelMart is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our platform at reelmart.in.

1. Who We Are

ReelMart is an e-commerce marketplace platform that connects Instagram sellers with buyers. ReelMart is operated by Pendam Suryamani (Proprietor), registered under Udyam Registration No. UDYAM-AP-21-0045720, based in Bapatla, Andhra Pradesh, India.

For privacy-related concerns, contact us at: support@reelmart.in

2. Information We Collect

From Buyers:

  • Mobile phone number (for OTP login)
  • Name and delivery address
  • Order history and transaction details
  • Device information and IP address
  • Payment details (processed securely by Razorpay — we do not store card/UPI details)

From Sellers:

  • Mobile phone number and email address
  • Business name and pickup address
  • Bank account details (for payouts)
  • PAN and GST details (for compliance)
  • Product listings, images, and descriptions
  • Sales history and earnings data

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To verify your identity via OTP login
  • To process orders and arrange delivery
  • To send order updates via WhatsApp and SMS
  • To process payments and seller payouts
  • To resolve disputes and provide customer support
  • To comply with legal obligations (GST, TCS)
  • To improve our platform and user experience
  • To detect and prevent fraud

4. Third Parties We Share Data With

We share your data only as necessary with trusted service providers:

  • Razorpay — payment processing and payouts
  • NimbusPost — shipping and delivery services
  • MSG91 — OTP and SMS delivery
  • Interakt (Meta/WhatsApp) — order notifications via WhatsApp
  • Supabase / AWS — secure data storage and hosting

We do not sell your personal data to any third party.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on secure servers hosted on AWS Mumbai (India). We use industry-standard encryption (SSL/TLS) for all data transmission. Access to personal data is restricted to authorised personnel only.

Payment information is processed entirely by Razorpay. ReelMart does not store your card numbers, UPI IDs, or banking credentials.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Opt out of promotional communications
  • Withdraw consent for data processing

To exercise any of these rights, email us at support@reelmart.in

7. Cookies

ReelMart uses essential cookies to keep you logged in and remember your preferences. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but this may affect platform functionality.

8. Children's Privacy

ReelMart is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via email or WhatsApp. The updated policy will be effective from the date of publication on this page.
